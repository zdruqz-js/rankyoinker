========================================================
  RankYoinker
  Ranks, stats and parties of every player - for VALORANT
  and League of Legends.   https://rankyoinker.de
========================================================

--------------------------------------------------------
1. GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------------
Nothing to set up. After installing, RankYoinker runs quietly in
the background and fills in everything on its own as soon as you
play.

Open it with the "RankYoinker" shortcut (Start menu or desktop),
or type  rankyoinker/  into your browser's address bar (with the
slash, otherwise the browser searches for it). If the short
address doesn't work:  http://localhost:1101

  VALORANT starts  ->  RankYoinker starts with it
  VALORANT closes  ->  RankYoinker closes with it

League of Legends is picked up automatically as well. You can
open the page before you play - it shows "Waiting for VALORANT"
until the game is ready.

--------------------------------------------------------
2. IN-GAME OVERLAY
--------------------------------------------------------
Small windows right on top of VALORANT: ranks and parties in
agent select, every player while you hold Tab, and your result
after the match. They never take focus away from the game.

Only visible in borderless fullscreen or windowed mode.
Settings (gear icon) > Game overlay: turn it off completely,
switch single parts off, pick the key, side and size, or show a
preview.

--------------------------------------------------------
3. ON YOUR PHONE
--------------------------------------------------------
Open the page on your phone while it's on the same Wi-Fi as
your PC. A device has to be paired once, so nobody else can
read along:

  1. Settings (gear icon) > "Pair phone"
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera
     (if that doesn't work, type in the address below it)
  3. Done - the phone stays paired

A QR code is valid for 5 minutes and for one device. The same
window lists all paired devices and lets you unpair them; this
only works on the PC itself. A paired phone can do everything
you can do on the PC, including dodging and instalock - only
pair it on your own Wi-Fi.

--------------------------------------------------------
4. QUITTING AND UNINSTALLING
--------------------------------------------------------
Usually you don't need to do anything.

Quit completely: right-click the RankYoinker icon at the bottom
right of the taskbar (if it isn't there, look under the small
^ arrow next to the clock) > "Quit program". To start it again,
use the "RankYoinker" shortcut.

Uninstall like any other app: Windows Settings > Apps. Autostart
and the short address are removed along with it.

--------------------------------------------------------
5. PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------
The page only talks to your own PC. The app talks to Riot's
servers (that's where ranks and stats come from) and to
rankyoinker.de: update check, a random install ID with app
version and language, your linked Riot account, usage counters
and error reports so bugs can be fixed. Nothing else.
Details: https://rankyoinker.de/#datenschutz

--------------------------------------------------------
6. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------
- Settings > the bug icon at the bottom ("Report a problem")
  sends a short description straight to us - you can check
  everything before it's sent.
- Help and questions: https://rankyoinker.de/discord
- Port 1101 (and 80 for the short address) must be free.
- Your settings, logs and caches live in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\RankYoinker - not in the program folder.
